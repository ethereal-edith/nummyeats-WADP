Join Our Team

Come cook, serve, and grow with us.

At Nummy Eats, our people are as important as our food. We are a team that takes pride in what we do, whether that is behind the grill, on the floor, or keeping things running behind the scenes. If you love good food and good company, there is a place for you here.

Kitchen Roles

Westlands Full-time
Line Cook

We need a cook who knows their way around Kenyan classics: ugali, stews, pilau, nyama choma. A Minimum 2 years' kitchen experience required. You'll work closely with Chef Patrick and be expected to maintain our standard on every plate, every shift.

CBD Full-time
Kitchen Assistant

Support our kitchen team with prep work, stock rotation, and keeping the kitchen clean and organised throughout service. No formal experience required. We will train the right person. A willingness to learn and a strong work ethic matter most.

Front of House

All Branches Full-time / Part-time
Waitstaff

Friendly, reliable, and comfortable working busy weekend shifts. No experience necessary. We train everyone from the ground up. What we need is someone who genuinely enjoys people and takes pride in making every customer feel looked after.

Westlands Part-time
Cashier

Handle payments, manage the till accurately, and be the last friendly face our customers see before they leave. Basic numeracy and comfort with mobile money (M-Pesa, card payments) is all we need. We will handle the rest.

Why Work at Nummy Eats?

  • Meals provided during every shift. Yes, from the real menu, not just the leftovers
  • Flexible scheduling that works around school and other commitments
  • A team culture that feels like family. We celebrate birthdays, wins, and milestones together
  • Clear growth paths: many of our supervisors started as waitstaff
  • Training provided for all roles, including those with no prior experience
  • Staff discounts across all three branches

How to Apply

Send your CV and the role you are applying for to careers@nummyeats.co.ke.
For waitstaff and kitchen assistant roles, you are also welcome to apply in person at any branch.
We review applications on a rolling basis and will reach out within 7 days.
