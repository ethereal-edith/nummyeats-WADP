About Nummy Eats

Rooted in tradition. Cooked with love.

Our Story

Nummy Eats opened its doors in Nairobi with a single branch and a single goal, which was to make authentic Kenyan cooking accessible without losing what makes it special. What started as a small family kitchen has grown into three branches across the city, but the recipes haven't changed.

We still cook nyama choma over charcoal. We still let our pilau simmer for hours. We still grind our own spices the way our grandmothers did, because we believe shortcuts show up on the plate. Every dish that leaves our kitchen carries the weight of that intention, that isfood made with care tastes different, and our customers feel that difference every time they sit down with us.

We are proud to have built something that feels like home to thousands of Nairobi families, and we are only getting started.

What We Stand For

Cooked Fresh, Every Day

Nothing sits in a freezer. Every meal is prepared the same day it is served — no shortcuts, no compromises.

Recipes That Remember

Our dishes are built on family recipes passed down through generations. The kind your grandmother would recognise.

The Same Experience, Every Branch

Whether you walk into our Westlands, Kilimani, or CBD branch, the food and the warmth are identical.

"To serve genuine Kenyan cuisine in a warm, welcoming space — one plate, one family, one celebration at a time."

How Far We Have Come

2019

Opened our first branch in Kilimani with a team of five and a menu of eight dishes.

2021

Expanded to Westlands following demand from customers who wanted Nummy Eats closer to the CBD.

2023

Opened our third branch in the Nairobi CBD and launched our reservations service online.

2025

Served our 100,000th customer and introduced a rotating seasonal specials menu.

2026

Planning our first branch outside Nairobi
