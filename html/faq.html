Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you visit.

Visiting Us

Both! Walk-ins are always welcome. That said, we recommend making a reservation if you are coming as a group of 5 or more, especially on weekends, since tables fill up fast. You can reserve online on our Reservations page.

For regular table bookings, 24 hours is usually enough. For large groups (10+) or special occasions, we recommend booking at least 3 days ahead so we can make sure everything is ready for you.

Lunch (12:00 PM – 2:00 PM) and dinner (6:30 PM – 9:00 PM) on Fridays and Saturdays are our busiest times. If you prefer a quieter experience, weekday mornings and early afternoons are your best bet. Walk-in wait times during peak hours can be 15–30 minutes.

Westlands (ABC Place) and Kilimani (James Gichuru Road) both have parking available on site. The CBD branch on Kimathi Street is street parking only so we recommend using a nearby parking silo if you are driving in.

Yes, all three branches have free WiFi for dine-in customers. Ask your waiter for the password when you sit down.

Payments & Delivery

We accept cash (KES), M-Pesa, and all major debit and credit cards. M-Pesa till numbers are displayed at each branch counter and on your bill.

Yes! we are available on Glovo and Uber Eats across all branches within their coverage areas. Search "Nummy Eats" on either app. Delivery times vary depending on your location and the platform's availability.
