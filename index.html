"From our hearts to your plates"

Nummy Eats started with one simple idea: Kenyan food deserves a seat at the table, served the way our grandmothers made it but in a space built for today. From sizzling nyama choma to a perfectly spiced pilau, every dish on our menu tells a story rooted in home. All from our easily accesible branches country wide.

Why choose us?

  • We always serve fresh food cooked on the same day. Nothing sits in the freezer waiting for an order
  • Our recipies are handcrafted and taste like home. Family recipies recognised by every kenyan
  • The same expereince at every branch, so you will always feel at home

Here is a sneak peak of our best selling meals

Beef Pilau : Pilau is a rich, fragrant rice dish that is heavily influenced by coastal Swahili culture. It is a staple at Kenyan celebrations (weddings, holidays, and Sunday gatherings), loved for its deep brown color and intense spice profile.

Ugali and Nyama choma : Ugali Nyama Choma is the undisputed king of Kenyan social life. Nyama Choma literally translates to "burned meat" or "roasted meat" in Swahili, and it is traditionally enjoyed at local joints alongside friends and family.
